NutriMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

This page explains how NutriMind handles your data in a clear and simple way. NutriMind is designed to keep your information under your control.

English

NutriMind stores your diary primarily on your device. If you voluntarily activate Social, only the social account data and categories you explicitly enable are uploaded to Firebase.

Data used by the app

  • Profile information you enter, such as gender, birth year, height, weight, goals and settings.
  • Diary entries such as weight, body fat, calories, macros, steps, active calories, training minutes and body measurements.
  • Optional Apple Health data, but only if you explicitly grant permission.
  • Optional purchase status for premium features via Apple In-App Purchase.

Apple Health

If you allow Health access, NutriMind reads selected health data to fill in your entries more automatically. Health data is only used inside the app to display and analyze your progress.

Social and Firebase

Social is optional. All health and fitness sharing starts disabled. When enabled, NutriMind stores your Firebase user ID, display name, friend code, consent record, friendships and requests. Confirmed friends can see only the categories you switch on. Chats and shared recipes, meals and workout plans are stored for both participants. They are encrypted in transit and at rest but are not end-to-end encrypted, so authorized project administrators can technically access cloud content. Firebase Authentication is processed in the United States.

Data sharing

NutriMind does not sell your data or use health and fitness data for advertising. Google processes optional Social data through Firebase on our behalf. Payments and purchase validation are handled by Apple.

Local storage

Your app data is stored locally on your device. If you export your data yourself, you decide where that exported file is stored.

Your control

You can edit, export, import or delete local data inside the app and revoke Apple Health permissions in iOS Settings. Social sharing can be disabled per category. Deleting the Social account removes the associated profile, sharing data, friendships, requests, chats and sent attachments.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@nutrimind.app
